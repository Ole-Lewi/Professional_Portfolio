My passion for technology was ignited during my high school years. However, not having

At ALX, I developed strong Backend Web Development skills including Python, MySQL, Django,

gained exposure to AI productivity tools used in production environments. Upon completion,
I was awarded three certificates: Certificate in Backend Web Development, Certificate in
AI Starter Kit, and Certificate in Professional Skills.

This experience sparked a deeper curiosity — I wanted to understand not just how to use
AI tools, but how to architect and engineer them from the ground up.

Following my graduation from ALX, I independently pursued Frontend Web Development using
online resources, YouTube tutorials and AI learning tools, gaining hands-on skills in
HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React — making me a fullstack-capable engineer.

I then systematically studied Machine Learning, covering the mathematical foundations:
Linear Algebra, Statistics and Probability, and Calculus. I progressed through Supervised
Learning, Unsupervised Learning and Deep Learning, training and evaluating models using
Scikit-learn and participating in Kaggle competitions to work with real-world datasets.
I further learnt to integrate ML models into APIs, work with HuggingFace models, and
built my first conversational AI system.

Driven by the vision of building intelligent systems that think, act and reason, I then
specialized in Agentic AI — enrolling at Ready Tensor School where I learnt to build
workflows, agentic systems and multi-agent systems using LangChain and LangGraph.
I trained agents to use custom knowledge bases, maintain memory, follow instructions,
and operate with Human-In-The-Loop(HITL) oversight. I also gained expertise in agent
evaluation using RAGAS and DeepEval frameworks.

A key aspect of my engineering philosophy is building systems that work under real
constraints — most of my development has been done on a Lenovo ThinkPad T470s with
limited RAM and intermittent connectivity. This shaped an offline-first approach:
building agents that switch gracefully between cloud APIs and locally-running models
depending on connectivity, ensuring systems degrade gracefully rather than fail completely.
